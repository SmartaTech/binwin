About Circular Country

Urban Problem. Indian Solution. Measurable Impact.

From Bengaluru, we’re starting with a simple belief: responsible waste disposal should be as easy as ordering a cab, as rewarding as using a loyalty app, and as measurable as a financial transaction.

Circular Country brings people, collectors, recyclers, brands and EPR ecosystems onto one platform.
