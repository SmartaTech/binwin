About Circular Country

Higher Purpose Alignment & Impactpreneurship

Circular Country Pvt.Ltd was founded on the belief that businesses should create value for society while protecting the planet.

We embrace Impactpreneurship—building commercially sustainable solutions that generate measurable environmental and social impact. Every initiative we undertake contributes to advancing India's transition toward a circular economy.

Our Team

Our multidisciplinary team brings together experts in waste management,
sustainability, technology, logistics, environmental science, marketing, and community engagement.

Together, we are creating innovative solutions that make circularity practical, scalable, and impactful.
